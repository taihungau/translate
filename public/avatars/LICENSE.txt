The talking faces on the course (woman.glb, man.glb)

Both are example avatars from the TalkingHead project by Mika Suominen
(https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead), compressed for phones (meshopt and 16-bit blendshape
deltas). woman.glb is also retouched: her jaw and lower cheeks are up to 12% narrower and her chin
6 mm longer (head mesh only, smooth falloffs, blendshapes kept), and her face texture is redone at
2048 px with smoothed skin, a warmer tone, contour and highlight, blush, eyeliner and eyeshadow,
defined brows and rose lips, glossy lips in the roughness map, fuller lashes, and the same warm
tone on the body's skin.

- woman.glb: TalkingHead's "avaturn.glb", created at Avaturn (https://avaturn.me) for
  non-commercial use.
- man.glb: TalkingHead's "avatarsdk.glb", created with Avatar SDK / MetaPerson
  (https://avatarsdk.com) for non-commercial use.

They are used here only in this personal, non-commercial English course. For any commercial use,
replace them with avatars you have the rights to (TalkingHead's README, Appendix A, lists ways to
make one, including the CC0 MPFB / MakeHuman route).
